Using AI to Improve Email Communication

Discover practical ways artificial intelligence can help you write clearer, more professional emails and manage your inbox efficiently.

How AI Can Help

AI tools can support everyday email tasks. They work best when you provide clear instructions and review the result before sending it.

✍️ Draft emails

Turn notes or ideas into a clear email with an appropriate greeting, message, and closing.

🎯 Adjust the tone

Make a message more formal, friendly, concise, or diplomatic depending on the recipient.

🔎 Proofread

Check spelling, grammar, clarity, and structure before sending.

🧾 Summarise messages

Identify the main points, deadlines, decisions, and actions in long email threads.

Example: A Professional Email

Suppose you need to ask a colleague for an update on a project. AI can help organise the request politely.

Subject: Request for Project Update

Dear [Name],

I hope you are well. Could you please share an update on the progress of [project name] when you have a moment?

Please let me know if you need any information or assistance from me.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[Your Name]

Useful Prompts to Try

  • “Write a polite email asking for an update on a task due this week.”
  • “Rewrite this email so it sounds professional, friendly, and concise.”
  • “Summarise this email and list the actions I need to take.”
  • “Check this draft for grammar and clarity without changing its meaning.”

Use AI Responsibly

  • Check names, dates, facts, attachments, and links before sending.
  • Make sure the final message reflects your meaning and voice.
  • Do not paste confidential or sensitive information into an AI tool unless you are authorised to do so and the tool is approved for that information.
  • Remember that AI can make mistakes; you are responsible for the email you send.
Tip: Give the AI the audience, purpose, key details, and desired tone. For example: “Write a short, polite email to my manager requesting a meeting next week.”

AI is an assistant, not a replacement for your judgement. Review every email before sending.
